What should I include?

Include taxes in the amounts you enter. Treat refundable deposits separately. Monthly minimums should reflect additional spending you would not make anyway. This comparison assumes every entered round is played at the member rate; reduce your round count if you often travel or play elsewhere.

Tee-time access, course quality, practice facilities, and guest privileges also matter. They aren’t assigned an invented dollar value here.
